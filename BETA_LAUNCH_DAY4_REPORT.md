## 💡 Key Learnings

1. **Website Design = Trust**
   - Professional landing page → Higher conversion

2. **Google Forms > Typeform (for free tier)**
   - No limits on responses
   - 100% free forever
   - Direct email integration

3. **Personalization Works**
   - Personal emails get responses
   - Generic posts get ignored

4. **Multi-channel approach**
   - Email, LinkedIn, Reddit, Twitter, communities
   - Diversified outreach = more sign-ups

5. **Speed matters**
   - Launched website same day as planning
   - Sent invites immediately
   - Momentum is everything
